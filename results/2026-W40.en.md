## Harness notes

- The multi-variant case (A-0676097b, 4 papers on one bundle) counts as one case and must pass every variant: 4/4 green for all three.
- Exam discipline: clean-room profile, no fallback chain, at most 2 papers at a time.
- Image v2 = v1 + PyYAML. The original scripts of A-d511f9e8 and A-a317e74b need PyYAML, which image v1 does not have, so they stop with an error. These two cases were re-taken on image v2 in all three tests. We compared the two images file by file: the 55 added files are all PyYAML, its library and their package records, and nothing else differs. No win or loss changed because of this; only the run time was affected.
- Paper gate off for the brand case A-d9b79b46: the grader copies the answer into a grading folder and builds it there, and does not clean it afterwards. The gate's file list also includes that folder, so the gate gave a false alarm. For this one case the gate was switched off in all three tests; the candidate cannot see that folder. Opus's answer was graded again in a clean grading folder: 12/12 passed, the same as the record, check by check.
- After the exam room was changed, one independent review was done. After it, two rules were fixed as NA: a timeout counts as NA under the rule we had at that time, and Fable's A-d9b79b46 is NA (on hold).
- Publication discipline: transcripts, oracles and candidate workspaces are never published.

## Verdict

All three Claude models are strong on the building side (coding, delivery, ops, requirements, convergence). The judging side (defense, attribution, review) is still a weak spot for all of them.

- **claude-sonnet-5-5**: 19'/24, with no holds apart from A-d511f9e8. It is the fastest of the three and uses fewer tokens than Opus and Fable. You can hand it review work, but have a person check its blocker list before acting on it. Defense and attribution work needs an independent check.
- **claude-opus-5-5**: 19'/24 in the W40 re-test; of its 3 NA, 2 come from the time limit and 1 is A-d511f9e8 (on hold for every lane). It is the only one of the three that passes review case A-47eea242, but its score on A-cdc3d11a is the lowest (−35). Read it next to its W39 result of 17'/24, and do not use the two to say it is stronger or weaker.
- **claude-fable-5-1**: 16'/24. It has the fewest false alarms in review, and its attribution score on A-a317e74b (14/15) is one check short of a pass. But it loses one ops case and one coding case, and the UI case is on hold until the question text is fixed and the case is taken again.
